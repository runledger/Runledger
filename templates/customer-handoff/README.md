# Customer handoff templates

Copy these files into the engagement's approved private workspace and replace placeholders with verified facts. Blank fields and `pending` are not completed evidence. Keep raw production data, credentials, and payment details out of public repositories.

1. [Intake](intake.md): identify the owner, application boundary, three scenarios, and data handling requirements.
2. [Scope](scope.md): agree one repository, one workflow, commercial terms, exclusions, and acceptance conditions.
3. [Evidence](evidence.md): capture commands, unchanged rules, broken/fixed results, redaction review, and CI configuration.
4. [Acceptance](acceptance.md): record the owner walkthrough, signoff, payment status, and use in the next two PRs.

Follow the [delivery guide](../../docs/customer-handoff.md). The $299 narrow setup and separately quoted hardening options are scope choices, not records of completed sales. These templates contain no customer testimonial or paid-delivery claim.
